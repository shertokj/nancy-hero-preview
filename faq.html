Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about therapy, insurance, and what to expect when working with me.

Getting Started

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule a free 15-minute consultation by calling me at (414) 736-6228, emailing yourtherapyconnection@gmail.com, or using the contact form on this website. During the consultation, we will discuss your needs and determine if we are a good fit.

What should I expect in the first session?

The first session is an intake where I gather information about your history, current concerns, and goals for therapy. It is also an opportunity for you to ask questions and get a sense of my approach. Come as you are - there is no need to prepare.

Do you offer in-person or telehealth sessions?

I offer both. Telehealth sessions are conducted via a secure video platform and are available to clients in Georgia, Illinois, and Wisconsin. In-person sessions are available at my office in Georgia.

Insurance & Payment

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. I am in-network with Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, United Healthcare, Oscar, and Oxford. I recommend contacting your insurance company before our first session to verify your mental health benefits.

What if you are not in-network with my insurance?

I can provide a superbill (detailed receipt) that you can submit to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement. Many plans cover 50-80% of session costs through out-of-network benefits.

What forms of payment do you accept?

I accept all major credit cards, debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and cash. Payment is due at the time of service.

Therapy Process

How long does therapy typically last?

The length of therapy varies depending on your goals and concerns. Some clients see significant progress in 8-12 sessions, while others benefit from longer-term support. We will regularly assess your progress and adjust as needed.

How often will we meet?

Most clients meet with me once a week, especially in the beginning. As you make progress, we may shift to bi-weekly or monthly sessions. Frequency is always based on your needs and preferences.

What is your cancellation policy?

I require 24 hours notice for cancellations. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice will be charged the full session fee. I understand that emergencies happen and will work with you on a case-by-case basis.

Confidentiality

Is everything I share confidential?

Yes, with a few legal exceptions. I am required by law to break confidentiality if there is risk of harm to yourself or others, suspected child or elder abuse, or if I receive a court order. We will discuss confidentiality in detail during our first session.

Do you share information with my doctor or family?

Not without your written consent. Your privacy is protected by law. If you would like me to coordinate care with your doctor or involve family members in your treatment, I will ask you to sign a release form.
