Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Don't see yours? Contact me.

Getting Started

What can I expect in the first session?
Our first session is about getting to know each other. I'll ask about what brings you to therapy, your history, and your goals. It's also your chance to ask questions and see if we're a good fit. There's no pressure - just an open conversation.
How long does therapy usually take?
It varies. Some people see progress in 8-12 sessions, others benefit from longer-term work. We'll discuss your goals and regularly check in on progress. You're always in control of the pace.
Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes! I offer a free 15-minute phone consultation so we can talk about what you're looking for and see if we're a good match. No obligation.

Logistics

What are your hours?
I offer flexible scheduling including evenings and weekends. Contact me to find a time that works for you.
Do you offer evening/weekend appointments?
Yes, I have evening and weekend availability. Just reach out and we'll find a time that fits your schedule.
How do telehealth sessions work?
We'll meet via secure video. You'll get a link before our session. All you need is a private space, internet connection, and a device with a camera. It's confidential and convenient.
What if I need to cancel?
I ask for 24-hour notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may be charged the full session fee. Emergencies happen - just let me know as soon as you can.

Payment & Insurance

What are your fees?
Individual therapy (50 min): $195
Couples therapy (80 min): $250
Free 15-minute phone consultation
Do you accept insurance?
I'm in-network with Aetna. For other insurances, I can provide a superbill for out-of-network reimbursement. Many clients receive 50-80% reimbursement.
What's a superbill?
A superbill is a receipt you submit to your insurance for reimbursement. I provide these after each session. Contact your insurance to learn about your out-of-network benefits.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit cards (Visa, Discover, American Express), HSA/FSA cards, Zelle, and Venmo. Payment is due at the time of session.

Therapy Process

Is everything confidential?
Yes, with a few legal exceptions. I can't share what we discuss without your permission. Exceptions include if there's immediate risk of harm to yourself or others, or if I'm required by law. We'll go over this in detail before our first session.
How will I know if therapy is working?
We'll set clear goals and check in regularly. You should feel heard, understood, and see progress toward your goals. If something isn't working, we'll adjust our approach.
What if I don't feel a connection?
The therapeutic relationship is crucial. If we're not a good fit, I'll help you find someone who is. It's important you feel comfortable with your therapist.
How often do we meet?
Usually weekly, especially at the beginning. As you make progress, we might meet less frequently. We'll find what works best for you.

Telehealth

What technology do I need?
A device with a camera and microphone (phone, tablet, or computer), a private space, and reliable internet. That's it!
Is telehealth as effective as in-person?
Research shows telehealth is just as effective for most issues. Many clients prefer it for convenience. I've been doing telehealth for years with great results.
Can I see you if I live in [GA/IL/WI]?
Yes! I'm licensed in Georgia, Illinois, and Wisconsin. As long as you're physically in one of those states during our session, we can work together.
What if I travel between states?
As long as you're in GA, IL, or WI during our session, we can meet. If you travel to another state, we'll need to reschedule.
