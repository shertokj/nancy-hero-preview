Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Your privacy is my priority. All information you share is kept strictly confidential and protected under HIPAA regulations. This policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information.

Information I Collect

I collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Health history and medical information
  • Insurance and payment information
  • Session notes and treatment records
  • Communications via email, phone, or contact forms

How I Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide psychotherapy services
  • Communicate about appointments and treatment
  • Process payments and insurance claims
  • Maintain clinical records as required by law
  • Comply with legal and ethical obligations

HIPAA Compliance

As a licensed psychotherapist, I follow the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) regulations. This means:

  • Your health information is protected
  • I cannot share your information without your written consent
  • You have rights regarding your health records
  • I use secure methods to store and transmit information

Confidentiality

Everything we discuss in therapy is confidential with limited exceptions:

  • If you pose an imminent danger to yourself or others
  • If I suspect child, elder, or dependent adult abuse or neglect
  • If required by court order (limited circumstances)
  • If you provide written authorization to release information

We'll discuss these exceptions in detail before our first session.

Data Storage and Security

I take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • Electronic records are encrypted and password-protected
  • Paper records are stored in locked cabinets
  • Secure video platforms for telehealth sessions
  • Regular security updates and best practices

Third-Party Services

I use the following services to support my practice:

  • Email: For communication (encrypted when possible)
  • Payment Processing: To process payments securely
  • Video Platform: For secure telehealth sessions
  • Form Services: For secure intake form submission

These services have their own privacy policies and security measures.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your health records upon request
  • Request corrections to your records
  • Know how your information has been shared
  • Request restrictions on certain uses of your information
  • File a complaint if you believe your privacy has been violated

Contact Forms

Information submitted through the contact form is sent directly to me via encrypted email. It is not shared with third parties. The form service (Formspree) may temporarily store data for delivery purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

This website uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. Clarity collects anonymous usage data. It does not collect personal health information.

No cookies are used for advertising or tracking purposes.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Continued use of my services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
