Privacy Policy

Your privacy is protected by law. This policy explains how I handle your personal and health information.

Last Updated: January 2026

Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

Your Information. Your Rights. My Responsibilities.
Your privacy is protected by state and federal law, including HIPAA.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Get a copy of your health and claims records
  • Ask me to correct your health records
  • Request confidential communications
  • Ask me to limit what I use or share
  • Get a list of those with whom I have shared your information
  • Get a copy of this privacy notice
  • Choose someone to act for you
  • File a complaint if you feel your rights are violated

How I Use and Share Your Information

I may use and share your information to:

  • Treat you by providing, coordinating, or managing your mental health care
  • Run my organization and contact you when necessary
  • Pay for your services through your health plan
  • Administer your health plan
  • Help with public health and safety issues
  • Do research under strict federal rules
  • Comply with the law
  • Address workers' compensation, law enforcement, and other government requests
  • Respond to lawsuits and legal actions

Confidentiality in Therapy

Everything you share in therapy is confidential with a few legal exceptions. I am required by law to break confidentiality if:

  • There is immediate risk of harm to yourself or others
  • I suspect child abuse or neglect
  • I suspect elder abuse or neglect
  • I receive a court order or subpoena

These exceptions are rare, and I will always discuss them with you if they become relevant to our work together.

My Responsibilities

I am required by law to:

  • Maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information
  • Follow the duties and privacy practices described in this notice
  • Not use or share your information other than as described here
  • Notify you if there is a breach of your health information

Changes to This Policy

I can change the terms of this notice, and the changes will apply to all information I have about you. The new notice will be available upon request.

Questions or Complaints

If you have questions about this notice or would like to file a complaint, please contact me:

  • Email: yourtherapyconnection@gmail.com
  • Phone: (414) 736-6228

You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights by sending a letter to 200 Independence Avenue, S.W., Washington, D.C. 20201, calling 1-877-696-6775, or visiting www.hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/complaints/.

I will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

Need Help?
If you have questions or concerns about your privacy rights, please contact me. Your trust is essential to our work together.
