Insurance

In-Network: Aetna

Out-of-Network Benefits: If you have out-of-network benefits, I can provide a superbill for reimbursement. Many clients receive 50-80% reimbursement.

Not sure about your benefits? Call your insurance and ask:
  • Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits?
  • What's my deductible?
  • What percentage is reimbursed?
  • How do I submit claims?
