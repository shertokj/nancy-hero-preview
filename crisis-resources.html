Need Immediate Help?

If you are in crisis or having thoughts of suicide, please reach out for help right now. You are not alone.

Call or Text 988

Crisis Resources

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential support for people in distress, 24/7.

Call or Text 988 988lifeline.org

Crisis Text Line

Text with a trained crisis counselor for free, 24/7.

Text HOME to 741741 crisistextline.org

Veterans Crisis Line

Confidential support for veterans and service members.

Call 988, Press 1 veteranscrisisline.net

The Trevor Project

Crisis intervention for LGBTQ youth.

1-866-488-7386 thetrevorproject.org

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Support for those experiencing domestic violence.

1-800-799-7233 thehotline.org

SAMHSA National Helpline

Treatment referral for mental health and substance use.

1-800-662-4357 samhsa.gov

If You Are in Immediate Danger

Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

  • Call 911 if you are in immediate danger
  • Call 988 if you are having suicidal thoughts
  • Go to your nearest emergency room
  • Reach out to a trusted friend or family member

Important: This page provides crisis resources for immediate support. I am not a crisis service and may not be available outside of scheduled sessions. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please use the resources above or call 911.
