Need Immediate Help?

If you are in crisis or having thoughts of suicide, please reach out for help right now. You are not alone.

Call or Text 988

If You Are in Immediate Danger

If you are in immediate danger or have a medical emergency, please call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

What to do right now:

  • Call 911 if you are in immediate danger
  • Call 988 if you are having suicidal thoughts
  • Go to your nearest emergency room
  • Reach out to a trusted friend or family member
  • Stay in a safe, public place until help arrives

Important: This page provides crisis resources for immediate support. I am not a crisis service and may not be available outside of scheduled sessions. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please use the resources above or call 911.
